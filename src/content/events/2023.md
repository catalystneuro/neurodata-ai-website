## About this year

NeuroDataReHack 2023 was held in Granada, Spain, as a satellite of the IBRO World Congress, with the goal of making the event accessible to participants who might not otherwise have the opportunity to attend. It drew 31 participants from 25 institutions in 15 countries, the most international cohort of any installment. A virtual Open Neurodata Showcase before the workshop let registrants meet the contributors behind the datasets on DANDI.

Following the event, participants were invited to apply for a Kavli Foundation Neurodata Discovery Award to continue projects begun at the workshop.
