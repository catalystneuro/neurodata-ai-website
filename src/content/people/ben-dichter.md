Ben Dichter is the founder and CEO of CatalystNeuro and directs the NeuroData AI Summer School. He is a core contributor to the Neurodata Without Borders ecosystem and to analysis and conversion tools built around it, and he has organized NeuroDataReHack every year since its first installment in 2022.
