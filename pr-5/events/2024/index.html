About this year

NeuroDataReHack 2024 was the first installment held at HHMI’s Janelia Research Campus, which has hosted the program every summer since. Twenty participants from 16 institutions completed 18 projects over five days, spanning surface-based parcellation of high-resolution imaging data, neural state space modeling of decision-making data, cross-species comparisons of hippocampal sequence dynamics, and early experiments with pre-training foundation models on NWB data.

Before the workshop, the virtual Open Neurodata Showcase let registrants meet the contributors behind the neurophysiology datasets on DANDI and explore virtual posters. Search API tutorials from this year are in the program GitHub repository.

Projects

Are cortical inhibitory neurons adjusting their cellular properties to local circuits?

Omar Valerio Minero

It is known that inhibitory neurons integrate into cortical circuits following the arrival of excitatory neurons.

Baseline Assessment of Cell Type Effects on Interpretable Neural Latent Dynamics

Keith Van Antwerp

How do opto-tagged cell types impact brain dynamics, using state-space modeling with a Poisson hidden Markov model as the baseline?

Building forecasting benchmarks for NeuroTask dataset

Carolina Filipe

Recent studies have shown that neural dynamics are preserved in animals performing different tasks, as well as in different animals performing the same task.

Comparison of spike sorting algorithms with SpikeInterface

Kate Doxey

Spike sorting is the process of extracting the activity of individual neurons from raw extracellular multi-electrode array recordings.

Dynamical modeling of decision-making through integrated behavioral and neural analysis

Luke Gong

Using dynamical systems to unravel the latent brain dynamics underlying decision-making processes.

Emotional and Cognitive Convergence during Naturalistic Movie Watching

Amber X. Chen

Investigate the impacts of emotional processing and cognitive engagement on memory retrieval, leveraging a multimodal dataset of single neuron, iEEG and fMRI data during movie watching and recall.

Estimating Partial Coherence Networks from Neural Spike Trains

Carla Pinkney

Statistically, neural spike trains can be represented by a multivariate point process whose second order structure can be characterised via the spectral density matrix.

Exploring Effective Pre-Training for Neuroscience Foundation Models with Neurodata Without Borders and DANDI

Yunlong Xu

Explore better pretraining tasks for foundation models in neuroscience, combining contrastive and masking pretraining on large-scale datasets.

Identification and distribution of prediction error-like signals in the mouse brain

Georg Raiser

Reward prediction error signals have classically been linked to dopaminergic neurons, but recent findings have uncovered similar signals from norepinephrine releasing neurons in the locus coeruleus.

Mapping spatial receptive fields in mouse visual cortex

Xinyue Ma

Benchmark methods for estimating spatial receptive fields from two-photon calcium imaging of visual cortical neurons.

Multiscale, Supervised Reanalysis of Optogenetically Induced Behavioral Reinforcement

Jack Goffinet

It has recently been shown that optogenetically evoked dopamine release in the dorsolateral striatum can reinforce the use of short behavioral syllables in the mouse.

Neural representation of ambiguity

Sophie Rogers

How does cue ambiguity impact the dynamics of choice formation across brain regions in the IBL Brain-wide Map dataset?

Pareto Optimality to Define the Topology of Brain States

Nils Nyberg

Leverage Pareto optimality to understand trade-offs in neuronal activity space.

Revisiting stability and plasticity in hippocampal sequence firing dynamics

Miles Martinez

Hippocampal place cells need to exhibit both stability and flexibility to support navigation through the world.

Smart neuromodulation approaches for motor control

Miguel Valencia

Developing smart electrical neuromodulation approaches for controlling motor disturbances related to Parkinson's disease.

Surface-based parcellation using high-resolution ex vivo 7 tesla MRI and microscopy imaging

Pulkit Khandelwal

MRI is the standard modality to understand human brain structure and function in vivo, and ex vivo imaging now opens the door to sub-millimeter study of brain structure.

The effect of probability on the structure of neural states

Yun Chen

Does the structure of preparatory neural subspaces in motor cortex geometrically represent reach in physical space, and does the probability distribution of targets change this map?

Understanding kinetics of neuropeptide release in presynaptic terminals

Luciana Gallo

CYbSEP is a novel tool to evaluate neuropeptide release by monitoring fusion of large dense-core vesicles.
