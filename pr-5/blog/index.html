Announcing the NeuroData AI Summer School

NeuroData AISummer School

A one-week residential school on reanalysis of open neurophysiology data and AI methods for neural data, held at HHMI's Janelia Research Campus. Formerly NeuroDataReHack.

Supported by the National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke of the National Institutes of Health under award number R25NS149357. The content is solely the responsibility of the authors and does not necessarily represent the official views of the National Institutes of Health.
