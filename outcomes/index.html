  • 2026: 17 of 22 respondents rated the six-day length as just right. 13 of 22 asked for more tutorials on using AI agents effectively.
  • 2025: 18 of 21 respondents rated the overall experience 5 out of 5 and the other three rated it 4. All 21 learned about community analysis and visualization tools, 20 learned how to read data from DANDI, 18 learned the NWB standard and its software, and 17 learned to use DANDI Hub. The NWB and DANDI training talks averaged 4.90 out of 5 and the dataset and tool lectures 4.48. 11 of 21 rated the five-day length as just right and 8 found it too short, which led to the six-day format in 2026.
  • 2024: Every survey respondent rated the overall experience 5 out of 5. 100% reported making connections with peers, 95% learned the NWB standard and community analysis tools, and 90% learned to use DANDI Hub for cloud-based analysis. 15 of 20 rated the five-day duration as ideal.

NeuroData AISummer School

A one-week residential school on reanalysis of open neurophysiology data and AI methods for neural data, held at HHMI's Janelia Research Campus. Formerly NeuroDataReHack.
