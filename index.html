NeuroData AI Summer School 2027 · July 19–24, 2027 · HHMI's Janelia Research Campus

Learn to reanalyze open neurophysiology data, from the people who built the tools.

A one-week residential school at HHMI-Janelia on working with NWB data on the DANDI Archive: finding it, streaming it, analyzing it with community tools, and applying AI methods to it. Free to attend, including lodging and meals. Formerly NeuroDataReHack.

What you learn

Two strands, taught side by side, and a week of project work to put them to use.

Foundations

Open data and the tools around it

How NWB files are organized and validated; how to search the DANDI Archive and stream from it instead of downloading; the flagship datasets from OpenScope, the International Brain Laboratory, and MICrONS; and the analysis ecosystem built on the standard: SpikeInterface and Kilosort, Pynapple and NeMoS, Neurosift, suite2p, Facemap, and Spyglass.

AI methods

Modern methods for neural data

Foundation models for neural activity and how to apply them across datasets; coding with large language models and AI agents; dimensionality reduction, neural decoding, and state space models; and how to judge whether a method developed on one lab's recordings transfers to another's.

Bring a question. Apply with a reanalysis project in mind. Teams form around shared datasets on the first day.
Mornings are lectures, afternoons are projects. Faculty and dataset stewards stay on hand all week.
Everything is published. Lectures are recorded and projects are documented in notebooks anyone can run.

The week at HHMI-Janelia

Everything is on campus

Participants stay in private rooms on the HHMI-Janelia campus, a few minutes' walk from the rooms where sessions run. Meals are provided, the kitchen handles dietary requirements, and the evenings belong to the pub, the pond, and the ping-pong table. For six days, the people who can answer your question are in the building.

The school is free to attend. Lodging and meals are covered by HHMI-Janelia, travel is reimbursed up to $550 per participant, and additional support can be requested.
